Signal → order → position

  1. Strategy signal: indicator conditions on a completed daily candle. Not an order.
  2. Simulated paper order: created only at the next 00:00 UTC open for signals that pass the caps; filled immediately at that open (+ slippage, + fee).
  3. Filled paper position: what the engine holds; marked to market every poll (unrealized P&L).
  4. Closed trade: exit fill recorded; P&L becomes realized.
